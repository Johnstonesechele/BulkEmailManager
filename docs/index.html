How to Install & Run

1
Click the Download button above to download the ZIP file.
2
Right-click the downloaded ZIP file and select "Extract All..." or use any unzip tool.
3
Open the extracted folder and double-click BulkEmailManager.exe to launch the app.
4
On first run, copy .env.example to .env.it and fill in your SMTP email credentials.

Contact Management

Import contacts from CSV files, merge multiple lists, remove duplicates, and filter by segment tags.

Email Templates

Create HTML and plain text templates with Jinja2 personalization. Preview before sending.

Bulk Campaigns

Send thousands of personalized emails with smart pacing, automatic retries, and Gmail block detection.

DKIM Signing

Optional DKIM authentication for improved email deliverability and sender reputation.

File Attachments

Attach up to 10 files per campaign. Manage attachments in the built-in Assets tab.

Bulk SMS

Send SMS messages via GSM modem. Auto-detect serial ports and manage message campaigns.

Surveys

Create surveys with text, multiple-choice, and checkbox questions. Track responses in the database.

Dark Theme

Beautiful Dark Blue and Gold interface designed for extended use without eye strain.

Quick Setup Guide

1
Open the Contacts (Email) tab and click "Load CSV" to import your contact list.
2
Go to Email Templates (Legacy) and click "Load HTML" to load your email template, or write one in the editor.
3
Switch to the Campaign tab, select your environment, enter a subject line, and click "Preview" to test.
4
When ready, click "Start Sending" to begin your campaign. Monitor progress in the Logs tab.
